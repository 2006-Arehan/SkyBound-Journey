Uttarakhand

The Land of the Gods — serenity, spirituality, lush valleys & the mighty Himalayas.

Top Experiences in Uttarakhand

  • Experience the divine Ganga Aarti in Haridwar and Rishikesh.
  • Trek to Valley of Flowers or embark on the Kedarnath yatra.
  • Unwind in the hill stations of Nainital and Mussoorie.
  • Go river rafting, bungee jumping & attend yoga retreats in Rishikesh.
  • Taste Pahadi specialties like Aloo Ke Gutke, Chainsoo, and Kafuli.
